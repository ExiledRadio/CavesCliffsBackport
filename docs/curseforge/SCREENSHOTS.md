# Caves & Cliffs Backport: screenshots

Taken in the Prism instance **Backport Showcase** (OptiFine), world `Caves and Cliffs Showcase`
(seed 650, Caves & Cliffs world type, y -64 to 319). Every scene is generated terrain with posed mobs: frozen (no AI), silent, invulnerable, and they stay
put. Paste the teleport command; it puts you on the scene's redstone block with the exact facing. Time, weather and
mob spawning are frozen in this world. Shaders on, F1 to hide the HUD. Save each as the name below; they get converted
to JPEG under 2 MB afterwards. Full notes per scene: `Releases/screenshot-scenes/SCENES.md`, scenes 34-43.

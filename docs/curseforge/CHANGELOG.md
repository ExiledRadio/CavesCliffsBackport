# 0.1.0 (beta)

First beta. Everything from 1.17 and 1.18 is in: the Caves & Cliffs world type with 1.18 terrain from y -64 to 319, deepslate, copper, amethyst, dripstone, lush caves, powder snow, candles, glow squid, axolotls, goats, 1.18's music, and its title screen. Seeds match 1.18.2.

Needs Backport Core 1.3.0 or newer.

Known limits in this beta:

*   The Caves & Cliffs world type turns itself off with OptiFine or Alfheim installed, and says so in the log. The 1.17 content still works. Support for both is being built.
*   Phosphor and Cubic Chunks also turn it off.

Back up worlds before trying it. Report bugs on the Discord.
